## Reflection

### 1. What went well during this unit?

I was able to settle on a clear concept for our app, StitchBoard, a crochet pattern and project tracker. Brainstorming it showed me that the idea covers the baseline requirements naturally: a one-to-many relationship between users and projects, a many-to-many relationship between patterns and materials, and full CRUD on projects. I also drafted the README, a feature list of seven features, and ten user stories using three roles, so our team has a solid starting point to review and refine together.

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

Our biggest challenge was scheduling. Our group hadn’t been able to meet before the deadline, so I drafted the Milestone 1 materials on my own to make sure we had something to submit. That meant the concept, features, and user stories reflect my ideas so far rather than a group decision. I let my teammates know that everything is open to change, and we plan to meet soon to review it together and decide on the final direction.

### 3. What additional support will you need in upcoming units as you continue to work on your final project?

I’d like guidance on designing the database schema, especially the join table with a unique field (the quantity of each material a pattern needs), and on structuring the Express backend and React frontend so that teammates can work in the same repo without conflicts. Help with using GitHub issues and project boards to split up work fairly across the group would also be valuable, since coordinating schedules has already been our hardest part.
